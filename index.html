About

Simulation you can own end to end.

F2C is a computational fluid dynamics product developed by Flow2C Simulation. Every part of it (the numerics, the solvers, the visualisation and the desktop application) is designed and written by our own team.

That lets us control quality, performance and licensing without compromise, and keep the whole product consistent from the first input to the final result.

Capabilities

What F2C does today

Fluid flow in 2D and 3D

Steady and transient incompressible flow for internal and external problems, with lift and drag reported on walls.

Heat transfer

Conduction and transport of heat and other scalar quantities through a domain.

Complex geometries

Run on meshes of real shapes imported from common meshing tools, not only on simple boxes, and export them again.

Trust in results

Every run checks its own setup, reports mesh quality, and explains itself when something does not converge.

Error estimates

Built-in mesh-independence studies tell you how accurate an answer is, not just what it is.

Desktop studio

Set up, run and inspect a case in one native application, with built-in 2D and 3D result views.

Verified continuously

Every change is checked automatically against reference solutions before it ships.

F2C Studio

From setup to result in one window.

  • Edit the case in a clear tree and settings panel
  • Run with one key and follow progress live
  • See fields, meshes and values the moment a run finishes
  • Turn 3D results around and look at them from any side
  • Open a case by double-clicking it
  • Results open in standard tools as well

Roadmap

Where F2C is going

  1. Available

    Foundations

    Heat conduction, scalar transport and incompressible flow in 2D, with the desktop studio and viewer.

  2. Available

    Trust in results

    Setup checks, convergence diagnosis, error estimation and continuous verification.

  3. Available

    3D and general meshes

    Flow in 3D on complex geometries and imported meshes, with 3D result views in the studio.

  4. Next

    Coupled simulation

    Several physical models and regions solved together in one case: the foundation for coupled heat transfer.

  5. Planned · Beta

    Heat transfer and turbulence

    Coupled thermal flows, heat transfer between solids and fluids, radiation and turbulence modelling. Opens a closed beta with design partners.

  6. Planned

    Geometry and meshing

    A geometry builder and mesher inside the studio, with CAD import.

  7. Planned

    Performance at scale

    Parallel execution for large models.

  8. Planned · Version 1.0

    First full release

    A signed installer, add-on modules, and chemical species transport.

  9. After 1.0

    Fluid–structure interaction

    Structures that deform under the flow, delivered as an add-on to existing installations.

  10. After 1.0

    Multiphase and beyond

    Two-phase flow, phase change, moving meshes, and a growing set of physical models.
